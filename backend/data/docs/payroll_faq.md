# Payroll FAQ

Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available in the HR portal from the 1st of the following month.

To change your bank account for salary credit, submit the bank change form in the HR portal. Changes take effect from the next payroll cycle.

Tax declarations for the financial year must be submitted by 31 January. Proof of investment is due by 15 February.

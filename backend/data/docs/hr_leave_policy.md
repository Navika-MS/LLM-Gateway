# Acme Leave Policy (2026)

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 8 unused days can be carried into the next year.

Sick leave is 12 days per year. A medical certificate is needed for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

To apply for leave, submit a request in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Your manager approves it within 2 working days.
